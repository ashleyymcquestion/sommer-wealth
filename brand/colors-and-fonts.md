# Colors and fonts

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dark | `#1b1815` | Main background (dark cards) |
| Dark (highlight) | `#2a2520` | Center of background glow |
| Cream | `#efe8dc` | Light backgrounds, QR tile (`#f3ece1`) |
| Gold | `#b98a5a` | Lines, icon circles, frames |
| Gold light | `#e2bf8f` | Highlights, small caps text on dark |
| Gold dark | `#8a5f35` | Shadows of gold gradients, gold text on light |
| Light text | `#e9e1d4` | Body text on dark |

Gold gradient (logo, headings): `#f1d7ad → #c99a68 → #9a6b3e`.

## Fonts (all free on Google Fonts)
- **Bodoni Moda**: "SOMMER" wordmark and the SWM monogram
- **Cormorant Garamond** (500/600, italic): names and headlines, e.g. "STRATEGY *today*"
- **Montserrat** (300/400/500): titles, contact details, small caps labels (wide letter-spacing)
