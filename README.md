# Sommer Wealth: Marketing Kit

Brand kit and marketing materials for Ashley McQuestion, Marketing & Lead Management at Sommer Wealth Management.

- `brand/`: logo mark, colors and fonts guide, original QR code
- `brand/fonts/`: Bodoni Moda, Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat (SIL Open Font License)
- `brand/icons/lucide/`: Lucide line icons (ISC License)
- `tools/`: `render-card.js` (rebuild card images and PDF), `make-qr.py` (brand-colored QR codes)
- `business-card/`: the current card (print-ready PDF, front/back images, and the editable source `card.html`)
- `business-card/options/`: earlier front designs that weren't chosen
